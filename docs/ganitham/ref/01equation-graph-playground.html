Before you close this page

In pairs

One of you sets the numbers without showing the screen. The other only sees the graph and has to say the equation out loud. Swap after three rounds.

Predict, then check

Before every drag, say what will happen: up, down, steeper, flatter, left, right? A prediction you got wrong teaches more than ten you got right.

The odd one out

Four of these five graphs are functions of x. One is not. Find it, and explain to your group what test you used.

Write it down

In one line each: what does the constant do, and what does the coefficient of x do? Keep those two sentences — every curve you meet later obeys them.
